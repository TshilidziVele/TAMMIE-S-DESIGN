Our Services

At Tammie's Design, we offer a comprehensive range of bespoke tailoring services shaped around your needs and preferences.

Women's Custom Tailoring

Create stunning, custom-designed garments that celebrate your style and personality.

Services Include:

  • Formal Wear - Elegant dresses and suits for professional and formal occasions
  • Casual Wear - Comfortable, well-fitted everyday garments
  • Evening Wear - Bespoke designs for special nights out
  • Business Attire - Professional skirts, blouses, and blazers
  • Traditional Wear - Cultural garments incorporating African textile traditions

Each piece is designed to flatter your unique body shape and personal style, with meticulous attention to fit and detail.

Traditional & Cultural Wear

Celebrate South African heritage with our stunning traditional designs.

Tshivenda & Regional Traditional Wear

Experience the beauty and elegance of South African traditional garments. Our expert tailors create stunning pieces that honour cultural heritage while ensuring perfect fit and modern comfort.

  • Tshivenda Attire - Vibrant colours and intricate patterns
  • Ndebele Designs - Geometric patterns and cultural authenticity
  • Shweshwe Collections - Premium fabric with traditional motifs
  • Ceremonial Garments - For weddings, coming-of-age, and celebrations
  • Cultural Accessories - Coordinated belts, wraps, and embellishments

Each piece is a celebration of your cultural identity, crafted with precision and pride.

Services Include:

  • Tailored Suits - Custom-made suits for business and formal occasions
  • Dress Shirts - Perfectly fitted shirts tailored to your measurements
  • Trousers - Bespoke trousers with precise tailoring
  • Casual Wear - Custom-designed casual shirts and trousers
  • Traditional Wear - Cultural and ceremonial garments

Our men's tailoring service ensures a perfect fit that enhances your professional appearance and personal style.

Our Design Process

From the first conversation to the final fitting, here is how your garment comes to life.

01

Initial Consultation

We meet with you to discuss your vision, preferences, and requirements.

02

Design Concept

We create initial design sketches and discuss options with you.

03

Fabric Selection

Together, we choose the perfect fabric to match your vision.

04

Measurements

Precise measurements are taken to ensure the perfect fit.

05

Sample & Fitting

An initial fitting is scheduled to check and adjust the garment.

06

Final Tailoring

We make final adjustments and complete the garment to perfection.

07

Delivery

Your custom garment is delivered, ready to wear.
